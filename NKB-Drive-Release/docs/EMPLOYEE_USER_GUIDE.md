# NKB Drive — Employee User Guide
## Getting Started with NKB Drive on Windows and macOS

NKB Drive gives you direct access to company files from File Explorer on Windows (Drive `N:\`) and Finder on macOS, working just like Google Drive for Desktop or a local hard drive.

---

## 1. Accessing Your Files

### Windows File Explorer
1. Open **File Explorer** (`Win + E`).
2. In the left navigation pane, look for **NKB Drive (N:)** featuring the golden NKB Cloud logo.
3. Open `N:\` to access your folders:
   - **My Drive**: Your personal employee workspace.
   - **Shared with Me**: Files and folders colleagues shared directly with you.
   - **Department Workspaces**: Dedicated folders for your assigned department (e.g. IT, Production, Accounting, HR).
   - **Company Shared**: Company-wide announcements, forms, and templates.

### macOS Finder
1. Open **Finder**.
2. Under **Locations** in the left sidebar, click **NKB Drive**.
3. All cloud files and department folders appear natively.

---

## 2. Working with Files

### Opening, Editing & Saving
- Double-click any document, spreadsheet, PDF, or CAD drawing to open it in Word, Excel, Acrobat, or AutoCAD.
- Edit and press **Ctrl+S** (or **Cmd+S**). Your changes are automatically synchronized back to NKB Cloud in the background.

### Offline Access
- Files you recently opened are cached locally on your computer.
- If you lose internet or network connectivity, you can continue working on cached files.
- Once connectivity is restored, your offline edits synchronize automatically.

### Conflict Handling
- If you and a teammate edit the same file simultaneously while offline, NKB Drive creates a conflict copy:
  `Filename (Conflict Copy - USERNAME - TIMESTAMP).ext`
- No data is ever lost or overwritten without warning.

---

## 3. System Tray / Menu Bar Controls

- Click the **NKB Cloud icon** in the Windows Taskbar Notification Area or macOS Menu Bar:
  - Check sync status ("All files up to date").
  - Click **Open in File Explorer** to jump directly to Drive `N:\`.
  - Click **Open Web Dashboard** to view version history, star items, or share files.
  - Click **Sync Now** to trigger an immediate check for updates.
